# Brand & Logo Motion Mode

## Load when

The task animates a logo, brand system, identity element, campaign title, product mark, or broadcast/digital bumper.

## Protect the identity

Treat supplied logo files, proportions, spacing, approved colors, and typography as constraints. Do not redraw, distort, retype, crop, or alter a brand mark unless the user explicitly requests a redesign and has authority to do so.

If only a low-quality reference is supplied, preserve its appearance as closely as practical and disclose any reconstruction.

## Discover motion behavior

Find a motion idea that expresses the brand's character: precise, playful, calm, energetic, technical, premium, human, or another quality grounded in the brief.

Consider how the mark assembles, reveals, resolves, interacts with its environment, or transitions into a message. Do not assume every logo needs particles, 3D extrusion, a light sweep, or a dramatic impact.

## Build a repeatable identity system

When the task includes a wider brand package, define reusable behavior for:

- logo entrance and exit;
- transitions and wipes;
- typography and title cards;
- color and background usage;
- supporting shapes and graphic devices;
- timing, easing, and sound cues.

Keep the logo itself recognizable and give it enough clean screen time.

## Production

Check vector fidelity, transparent edges, aspect ratio, scaling, color management, background contrast, and export requirements. Confirm whether alpha transparency or multiple aspect ratios are needed.

## Quality check

Is the brand instantly recognizable? Does movement express a relevant brand quality? Is the result distinctive without damaging the mark? Does it work at the real delivery size and duration?
