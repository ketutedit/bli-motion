# Product UI Motion Brief

- **Product:** Fictional productivity app named Northstar.
- **Audience:** General productivity-app users.
- **Purpose:** Show a clear path from sign-in to workspace.
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (360 frames at 30 fps).
- **Frame:** 1920×1080.
- **Required states:** Login form, sign-in feedback, loading, dashboard.
- **Art direction:** Calm, editorial, warm neutral UI with muted green as the main product color.
- **Accuracy:** All brand, data, and interface content is fictional and illustrative.
- **Delivery:** Remotion composition and MP4.

## Key communication point

The viewer should understand the sequence of interface states without the motion obscuring the form or dashboard.
