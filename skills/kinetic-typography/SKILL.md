# Kinetic Typography Skill

## Purpose
Use animated type to communicate meaning, hierarchy, rhythm, and tone.

## Hierarchy
Define roles for:
- headline / key phrase,
- supporting sentence,
- labels and annotations,
- numbers and data,
- captions or subtitles.

Use size, weight, position, contrast, and timing to distinguish roles.

## Animation choices
Match movement to meaning:
- scale or weight change for emphasis,
- tracking change for expansion or tension,
- directional movement for progression,
- masking for reveal,
- word-by-word sequencing for spoken emphasis,
- line movement for connection or grouping.

## Readability
- Keep phrases concise.
- Avoid excessive movement while the viewer is reading.
- Maintain strong contrast and adequate size for mobile.
- Avoid placing important text over busy imagery.
- Keep line breaks intentional.
- Do not animate every word unless the format and pacing justify it.

## Synchronization
Use narration as a timing reference, but do not force exact word-level synchronization if it harms readability. Reveal key phrases at the moment they become meaningful. Allow a readable hold after important text appears.

## Avoid
- random letter scattering,
- constant pulsing,
- unnecessary rotation,
- overly tight tracking,
- rapid word changes that cannot be read,
- using motion as a substitute for a clear sentence.
