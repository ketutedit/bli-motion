# Benchmark 02 — Brand and Logo Reveal

## Exact prompt

Create a 6-second, 1920×1080, 30 fps brand reveal for a fictional design studio named NORTHSTAR. Create a simple typographic or geometric identity for the name as part of the motion piece, then animate its reveal into a clean final lockup. The visual direction should communicate a confident, contemporary creative studio. Make the reveal memorable through composition and motion design rather than relying on a generic particle burst or lens flare. Keep the name correctly spelled and readable.

Deliver a runnable Remotion project and an MP4 render. Use only dependencies in the shared blank benchmark project. Do not use external images, paid assets, or network-loaded fonts.

## Evaluation focus

- Strength and relevance of the visual concept.
- Identity clarity and typography.
- Motion timing, reveal logic, and final hold.
- Cohesion of color, shape, and composition.
- Correct 6-second duration and stable render.

## Controlled conditions

Run the exact prompt for A, B, and C with the same model, settings, blank project, dependencies, and available tools. Do not give only one condition extra assets or implementation help.
