# Benchmark 01 — Product UI Motion

## Exact prompt

Create an 8-second, 1920×1080, 30 fps motion-graphics video for a fictional productivity app named Luma. The video should introduce a polished login experience: show the app identity, email and password fields, a clear sign-in action, a short loading state, and a successful arrival at a simple dashboard. Make the interaction understandable and visually distinctive. Do not copy a real product interface. Use a coherent art direction chosen for this product; avoid generic dashboard filler and decorative motion that does not support the interaction.

Deliver a runnable Remotion project and an MP4 render. Use only the dependencies already present in the shared blank benchmark project. Do not use external images, paid assets, or network-loaded fonts.

## Evaluation focus

- Interface hierarchy and product credibility.
- Clarity of field, button, loading, and success states.
- Quality of transitions and micro-interactions.
- Distinctive art direction without sacrificing usability.
- Correct 8-second duration and stable render.

## Controlled conditions

Run the exact prompt for A, B, and C with the same model, settings, blank project, dependencies, and available tools. Do not give only one condition extra assets or implementation help.
